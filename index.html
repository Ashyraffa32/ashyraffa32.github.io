❓ I have a copy of OMORI. How do i install the font?

Simply do this:

  1. Open Steam and go to your Library.
  2. Right-Click OMORI.
  3. Hover over Manage and click Browse local files.
  4. Once the folder pops up, just head into www folder, and then go to fonts folder.
  5. Click "OMORI_GAME.ttf" and "OMORI_GAME2.ttf", it'll launch a Notepad, then click Install.
  6. To apply your changes in the website, restart your browser and go to this website again.
