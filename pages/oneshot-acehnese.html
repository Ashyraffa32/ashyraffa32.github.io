About This Mod:

This is a translation mod for OneShot in Acehnese language. To know more about Aceh, click here.
To know more about the mod, scroll the page.

You may ask. Why i made this?

Acehnese is my mother tongue, therefore, this language was threatened with extinction. I tried to preserve it by creating this translation mod for Acehnese for the game OneShot.

For data about the extinction status of the Acehnese language, click here.

"I'm interested! Where do you guys update your progress?"
